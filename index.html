How to Password Protect Hidden Photos on iPhone — Complete Guide

Whether you want to lock your gallery from snooping friends or secure sensitive images completely, here is exactly how to password protect hidden photos on iOS using built-in tools and dedicated secure vaults.

Quick Answer

Can you password protect hidden photos on iPhone? Yes. If you are running iOS 16 or later, your iPhone's "Hidden" album is automatically locked behind Face ID, Touch ID, or your device passcode. What You Need to Know Before Starting

Knowing how to password protect hidden photos on iPhone has changed dramatically over the last few iOS updates. Previously, putting a photo in the "Hidden" album simply moved it out of the main camera roll, but anyone scrolling to the Albums tab could still open it. It wasn't actually protected.

Today, there are two primary ways to lock hidden photos iphone users rely on:

  1. The Built-in OS Method: Relying on iOS 16+ which automatically links the Hidden Album to your Face ID or device passcode.
  2. The Vault App Method: Using a dedicated app to set a completely different password specifically for sensitive photos.

OS Compatibility & Requirements

  • For Native Locking: Your iPhone must be updated to at least iOS 16. Older versions do not support biometric locking of the Hidden album.
  • For App Vaults: Works on any iOS version supported by the App Store (generally iOS 12+). Requires downloading a trusted third-party app.

Lock Hidden Photos with Face ID on iPhone (Built-in)

If you have iOS 16 or later, Apple finally added the ability to lock your Hidden and Recently Deleted albums. This is the fastest method, as it requires no extra software.

Step-by-Step Instructions

  1. Open the Settings app on your iPhone.
  2. Scroll down and tap on Photos.
  3. Find the toggle that says Use Face ID (or Touch ID, depending on your model). Toggle it ON.
  4. Ensure the toggle for Show Hidden Album is also turned ON (otherwise you won't be able to find the album to access it).
  5. Open your Photos app, select the photos you want to hide, tap the three dots in the top right, and select Hide.

How to Add an Extra Password to iPhone Hidden Photos

A major security gap with the native iOS method is the shared passcode. If your partner, child, or a snooping acquaintance knows the 6-digit PIN to unlock your phone, they can automatically unlock your hidden album too.

To truly password protect a photo album on iPhone independently, you need to use a secure vault app that isolates the photos from the iOS ecosystem.

Why Use a Secure Vault?

  • Separate Credentials: Set a different password for hidden photos iphone doesn't know about.
  • Decoy Passwords: Advanced apps let you set a fake PIN that opens an empty vault if you are forced to unlock it.
  • Break-in Tracking: Some apps take a photo using the front-facing camera if an incorrect PIN is entered.
